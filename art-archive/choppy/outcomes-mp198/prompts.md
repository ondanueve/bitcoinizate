28 images, in priority order. Generate with ChatGPT first (free plan, project 'Choppy bitcoin'), then Google AI Studio free models when ChatGPT hits its limit.

How to use: open a new chat, upload the listed reference images in the stated order, paste the prompt, ask for a landscape 2.4:1 image. Download it, then run:

`/workspace/arc-outcomes/postprocess.sh <downloaded-file> chance/<id>-<key>-wide.jpg`

Review each result at 480x200 and phone width. Reject if: Choppy's ₿ or headband is wrong or the coin is distorted, any visible text/numbers, Marek in sporty clothes, Uncle Mike looking like the mustached uncle, Lena in an island scene, a readable chess position.

Key scheme (game lookup: `<opt>-<variant>` -> `<variant>` -> `<opt>`): casino/poker/startup/cousin/usedcar/landfill outcomes are keyed by variant (win/lose/bust/works/lemon/nokia/nothing) and `c` for the no-roll option; unclemike/stateVisit/extensionCord/baby/nicoWedding by option letter; chess by `win`/`miss`. Poker scoop, 3x and min-cash share one `win` image. Landfill jackpot (0.03%) intentionally has no art.

Reference files (all on the box): `/workspace/arc-outcomes/refs/` (choppy.png, marek.png, marek-dressed.jpg, nico.png, paco.png, mike.jpg, lena.jpg, ortega.jpg, style-citadelProblem-wide.jpg).
