Privacy policy

Bitcoinizate is a browser games site. We do not sell a token, we do not run ads, and we do not sell your data.

If you play as a guest, this device keeps your best score, awards, and settings such as look, sound, and language. That stays in local storage on the device. We do not receive it.

If you create an account we keep your email, a password hash (handled by Supabase), your alias, optional Bitcoin and Lightning payout addresses, your cloud high score, a saved run tape, awards, and your versus rating. Feedback you send while signed in is stored with your account.

We use that to sign you in, show a public leaderboard of alias and score, pay prize winners, and read feedback. We do not sell lists. Donations go to our Lightning invoice or Bitcoin address. The payment itself does not tell us who you are.

You can sign out anytime. To delete an account, email hello@bitcoinizate.com from the address you registered with. We delete the account and the profile tied to it.

Questions: hello@bitcoinizate.com
